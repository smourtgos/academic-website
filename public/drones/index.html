Drone Misuse Tracker

Media-reported incidents of non-state malicious, criminal, and reckless drone use worldwide since January 2020. The data are built to inform research on counter-drone measures in criminology and criminal justice.

TrendCategoriesMapIncidents Methods & limitations
Read before using: these counts are of media-reported incidents. They measure how often news outlets (mostly English-language) reported drone misuse, not how often it happened. A model coded each incident from news text, and not every record has been checked by a person (see each record's review status). Methods & limitations.
